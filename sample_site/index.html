How a guided day works

  1. Meet your guide at the Silver Fork trailhead at 7:30 AM for a gear check.
  2. Hike at a pace set by the slowest member of the group, with a break every hour.
  3. Lunch at a scenic stop, with a short talk on local geology and wildlife.
  4. Return to the trailhead by mid-afternoon, before the usual afternoon storms.

Trail safety briefing

Every hiker carries at least two litres of water, a rain shell and a headlamp, even on short routes.

Weather above the tree line changes quickly. If your guide calls a turnaround, the whole group turns around, no exceptions.

Stay on marked paths to protect alpine plants, and pack out everything you bring in.
