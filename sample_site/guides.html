Meet the guides

All of our guides hold a wilderness first responder certificate and have spent at least three full seasons in the Silver Fork range.

Maya Okafor

Lead guide. Maya has led more than four hundred summit days and teaches our navigation workshop.

Tomas Reyes

Naturalist guide. Tomas studied alpine botany and runs the wildflower walks every July.

Ines Larsen

Winter specialist. Ines guides our snowshoe programme and holds an avalanche safety level two certificate.

How we train our guides

New guides shadow a lead guide for twenty trips before leading a group of their own.

Every spring the whole team completes a two-day refresher on rope work, rescue scenarios and group management.
