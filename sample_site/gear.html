Gear checklist

Pack light, but pack right. Open each section to see what to bring.

Clothing
  • Waterproof rain shell with a hood
  • Warm mid layer such as a fleece or light down jacket
  • Broken-in hiking boots with ankle support
Food and water
  • At least two litres of water per person
  • A packed lunch and high-energy snacks
Safety
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • Sun hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Personal medication and blister plasters

Rental gear

Trekking poles and gaiters can be rented at the trailhead.
